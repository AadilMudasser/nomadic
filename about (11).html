Our Philosophy

A sanctuary
of creativity.

At Nomadic, we believe beauty is far more than appearance. It's confidence, self-expression and the freedom to feel completely yourself.

We've created a space where artistry, education and genuine connection come together, where every experience is approached with intention and every person is treated as an individual. No trends to follow. No one-size-fits-all approach. Just thoughtful craftsmanship, honest expertise and exceptional care.

Our team shares a passion for creating meaningful experiences built on creativity, trust and continuous learning. We believe the best results come from understanding the person before the service, taking the time to listen, guide and create something that feels authentic to you.

Whether you're visiting for a subtle refresh or a complete transformation, our purpose remains the same: to help you leave feeling more confident, more empowered and more connected to yourself than when you arrived.

This is Nomadic.

Where individuality is celebrated, artistry is respected and beauty is created with intention.

Home Service & Out-of-Hours Appointments

Home visits and appointments requested outside of our regular opening hours may be available upon request and are subject to an additional fee. Please contact our team to discuss availability and pricing.

Booking Policy

To secure selected appointments, a deposit is required at the time of booking.

These appointments are reserved exclusively for you and are often in high demand. Deposits help us protect our specialists' time while allowing clients on our waiting list the opportunity to book should an appointment become available.

Your deposit will be deducted from your final bill and is fully refundable up to 24 hours before your appointment.

Please Note

  • Your appointment is not confirmed until your deposit has been received.
  • Appointments are allocated on a first come, first served basis and remain available until the required deposit has been paid.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Cancellations made more than 24 hours before your appointment will receive a full refund of the deposit.
  • Cancellations made within 24 hours, including on the day of your appointment, will result in the deposit being non-refundable.
  • If you reschedule within 24 hours, 50% of your deposit will be forfeited. The remaining 50% will be transferred to your new appointment.

Late Arrivals

We understand that delays can happen and kindly ask that you let us know if you're running late.

We offer a 15-minute grace period wherever possible.

Arriving more than 15 minutes late may reduce the time available for your appointment or require us to adjust or reschedule your booking to maintain the quality of your experience while respecting the time of guests booked after you.

Thank you for helping us create the best possible experience for every guest.
